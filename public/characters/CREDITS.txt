Character models, hairstyles, textures and animation clips in this folder are derived from:

  Universal Base Characters (Standard)   by Quaternius   https://quaternius.com
  Universal Animation Library (Standard) by Quaternius   https://quaternius.com

Both are released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

They were simplified, re-packed and re-textured by tools/characters/build.mjs and
tools/characters/atlas.py. The clothing (hoodie, shirt, T-shirt, kurta) is generated
in src/characters.js and is original to this project.
